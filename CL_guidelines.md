## General Instructions for the Cover Letter

Do not use a pompous language.
Do not exagerate the match between my CV and the job description
Emphasize, if suitable, that the job coincides with the direction in which I want to steer my career from now on.
If the job is non academic, you can mention that I am looking for more applied fields, away from the funding+publish cycle in academia.
If the job is close to consulting, emphasize my previous experience connecting research with local enterprises, institutions and government.
You can emphasize my analytical math oriented mind, with skills in programming.
If related, you can mention my pasion for any of the following Economics, International relations (avid news reader), Languages (spoken and coding), Art, Diving and free diving, Running (have done some half marathons), Social activism (have organized ecological activities as beach clean ups)
